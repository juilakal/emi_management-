Features & Capabilities

Designed for Complete Financial Visibility

Built with robust banking calculations, strict security standards, and an intuitive user interface.

Reducing Balance Calculator

Accurately calculates monthly installments using standard financial compound formulas: \(P \cdot r \cdot (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)\).

Itemized Amortization Schedules

Generates monthly breakdowns isolating principal and interest components with proper month-end date progression and penny reconciliation.

Payment Tracking & History

Record partial and full payments across Bank Transfer, UPI, Cash, and Other methods with validation against overpayments.

Overdue Detection

Dynamic evaluation checks system dates against installment due dates to highlight overdue obligations before penalties occur.

Strict Isolation & Security

Werkzeug password hashing, session cookies, CSRF protection, and multi-tenant authorization preventing any unauthorized data access.

Interactive Analytics & CSV Export

Visual Chart.js diagrams tracking paid vs pending ratios, loan balances, and one-click sanitized CSV exports of payment ledgers.
